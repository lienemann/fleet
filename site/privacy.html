What is recorded

GitHub serves the page and records the IP address of every visitor in its server logs, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The page sends nothing anywhere else: the fonts and pictures come from the same server, and there are no cookies, analytics or counters.

If you switch between the light and the dark theme, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage. It stays on your computer.
